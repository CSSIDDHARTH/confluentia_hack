shelfSense AI: Autonomous Retail Compliance Agent
The Challenge
• Retail stores face operational and revenue losses due to misplaced products, incorrect shelf 
labels and prices, stock-outs, and deviations from planned shelf layouts. Manual shelf audits 
are time-consuming, error-prone, and difficult to scale.
• Build an AI-powered retail compliance system that analyses shelf images or videos to 
automatically detect merchandising and compliance issues, helping store managers maintain 
accurate, well-organized, and adequately stocked shelves.
The Solution Should Be Able To
• Validate product placement and planogram compliance: Detect shelf products, compare 
their placement and arrangement against the expected planogram, identify missing or 
misplaced products, recommend correct locations, and generate a compliance score.
• Verify product labels and prices: Match products with their corresponding shelf labels, 
extract displayed prices using OCR, compare them against a reference product catalogue, and 
flag product-label or pricing mismatches.
• Detect shelf availability issues: Identify empty shelf spaces, low-stock situations, and 
products requiring replenishment.
Expected Output
• The solution should generate a clear report or dashboard highlighting detected violations, 
shelf availability issues, recommended corrective actions, and an overall planogram 
compliance score.
• Additional points will be awarded to teams that successfully deploy or run inference on an 
edge device or in an edge-simulated environment.

Common Ask
Before building the solution, teams are expected to research and understand the background of the 
given use case, including the real-world context, users, workflows, and key challenges involved. 
Since problem statements may not specify every detail, teams should make reasonable and valid 
assumptions wherever required and clearly state these assumptions as part of their solution. Teams 
are encouraged to think beyond the requirements provided, but any additional features, data, 
workflows, or assumptions should be realistic, relevant, and supported by their research.
Bonus Considerations
Additional points may be awarded for:
• Efficient SLM Usage: Teams may use an LLM of any size. However, additional points may 
be awarded for effectively using a Small Language Model with fewer than 20 billion 
parameters, where appropriate, while maintaining strong performance and reducing latency or 
resource requirements.
• Additional Innovation: Beyond the capabilities specified in each challenge, teams that 
introduce relevant additional features, workflows, or domain-specific innovations that 
improve the solution’s usefulness, robustness, or real-world applicability may receive 
additional points
